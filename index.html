Interaction

One Pill. Total Control.

The dashboard is the heart of mi. Browser. A single swipe up on the Pill reveals your entire digital world—History, Bookmarks, and Settings—all logically organized in an elegant, glass-morphism sheet.

  • Swipe up to open, down to hide
  • One-handed optimization
  • Haptic-feedback gestures
  • Immersive full-screen mode
Organization

Visual Tab Management.

Stop guessing which tab is which. mi. provides a high-fidelity grid view with real-time snapshots of your open sessions, making it effortless to switch context or clean up your workspace.

  • Visual snapshot previews
  • Seamless reordering
  • Clear-all tabs shortcut
  • Hyper-fast tab switching
Aesthetic

Deeply, truly yours.

Browser settings shouldn't be boring. mi. offers a robust customization engine that lets you adjust UI density, pill height, and corner radius. Change your accent color and watch the entire app adapt instantly.

  • Real-time theme engine
  • Adjustable corner geometry
  • Custom search engine support
  • UI density & font scaling
Utility

Power in your pocket.

The context menu provides instant access to the tools you use most. From built-in QR code generation and scanning to desktop mode toggles and a clean Reader View, everything is just a long-press away.

  • Integrated QR Toolbox
  • One-tap Reader Mode
  • Desktop/Mobile toggling
