Interaction

One Pill. Total Control.

The Pill is the heart of mi. Browser. Tap to search with live suggestions, swipe sideways to go back or forward, swipe up for the Tab Switcher, and swipe down to hide it.

  • Swipe up for tabs, down to hide
  • One-handed optimization
  • Pin the recall button left, center, or right
  • Immersive full-screen mode
Organization

Visual Tab Management.

Swipe up and the page zooms out into a card, just like your phone's recent apps. Your tabs line up by when you last used them, with live previews. Swipe a card up to close it.

  • Zooms with your finger
  • Most recent tab on the right
  • Swipe up to close, with undo
  • Live previews of every tab
Aesthetic

Deeply, truly yours.

Browser settings shouldn't be boring. mi. offers a robust customization engine that lets you adjust UI density, pill height, and corner radius. Pick an accent (or use your wallpaper) and the whole Material 3 color scheme regenerates instantly.

  • Material You dynamic color
  • Adjustable corner geometry
  • Rounded Google Sans Flex option
  • UI density & font scaling
Utility

Power in your pocket.

The tab menu puts the tools you use most a tap away: favorite, bookmark or pin the tab, follow a product's price, switch desktop site or Reader mode, find in page, and scan or create QR codes. It's the ⋮ in the Tab Switcher.

  • Price following, on your phone
  • Integrated QR Toolbox
  • One-tap Reader Mode
  • Desktop/Mobile toggling
  • Native Print & Share
