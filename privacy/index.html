Privacy Policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

mi. Browser does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Everything you do in mi. stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Your bookmarks, favorites, history, recent searches, open tabs, settings, site icons and tab previews are stored only in mi.'s private storage on your phone. They are never uploaded. You can delete them at any time with Settings → Privacy & Security → Clear Browsing Data, Settings → Backup & Reset → Wipe All Data, or by uninstalling mi.

Incognito tabs store nothing: they aren't added to history or recent searches, aren't restored, and their cookies, logins and site data are erased when you close them.

Exporting your settings creates a file only where you choose to save it.

Connections mi. makes

Like any browser, mi. connects to the internet on your behalf. These are the only connections it makes:

  • Websites you visit, and those same websites for their site icons.
  • Your search engine when you search. If Search Suggestions is on, what you type in the search drawer is sent to your search engine as you type, to get suggestions. You can turn this off; it is off in incognito tabs by default and never used with a custom search engine.
  • GitHub (api.github.com), to check whether a new version of mi. is available, and to download the update if you choose to install it.
  • Filter list providers (easylist.to and secure.fanboy.co.nz), to download updated ad, tracker and cookie-banner blocking lists about once a week on Wi-Fi, or when you tap Update Filter Lists. Nothing about your browsing is sent.
  • Google Safe Browsing, through Android System WebView, which may check pages against Google's list of dangerous sites to warn you about them.

These services receive your IP address and basic device information, as with any web request, under their own privacy policies. The websites you visit are also governed by their own privacy policies; mi. blocks known ads, trackers and cookie banners by default to limit what they can collect.

Permissions

mi. only asks for a permission when you use a feature that needs it:

  • Camera: scanning QR codes, and websites you allow (for example, video calls).
  • Microphone: websites you allow.
  • Location: websites you allow, asked site by site.
  • Notifications: the notification that closes all incognito tabs.
  • Install apps: installing mi. updates you choose to download.
  • Passkeys: letting Android's Credential Manager sign you in to websites with passkeys.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older): saving downloads.

Passkeys, saved passwords and payments are handled by Android and the provider you choose (for example, Google Password Manager or Google Pay), never stored by mi.

Children

mi. does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
