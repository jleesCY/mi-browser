## Short description

A private, gesture-driven browser built around one Pill.

## Description

mi. Browser is a simple, private browser built around one idea: everything you need is in a single Pill at the bottom of the screen. Search, switch tabs and go back with a swipe, while ads and trackers are blocked for you.

• Extensions made for phones, from Firefox Add-ons: Dark Reader, uBlock Origin, SponsorBlock, Privacy Badger and more.
• Ads, trackers and cookie banners blocked by default, with tracking-free links and HTTPS Only.
• Follow a product's price and get told when it drops, goes on sale or comes back in stock.
• Keep any site on your home screen as its own app.
• Incognito tabs that keep nothing and vanish when you close them.
• Material 3 Expressive design, coloured by your wallpaper or your choice of accent.

No accounts, no tracking.
