The Complete User Guide

A comprehensive deep-dive into every feature, setting, and interaction within mi. Browser.

Pill Interactions

mi. Browser is designed for one-handed use. Everything centers around "The Pill" at the bottom of your screen.

Tap the Pill

Focuses the address bar. Type a URL or a search query to navigate instantly.

Swipe Up

Reveals the Dashboard. This is your hub for History, Bookmarks, and System Settings.

Swipe Down

Hides the Pill entirely for an immersive full-screen experience. Tap the floating recall button to bring it back.

Search Engine Only

Tap the search engine icon inside the address bar to force search mode. This bypasses URL parsing and sends your query directly to your search engine.

Navigation Gestures

Swipe Right

Go back to the previous page in your history.

Swipe Left

Go forward to the next page if you've navigated back.

Pro Tip: These gestures work directly on the Pill area, allowing for effortless one-handed browsing.

The Dashboard

Swipe up on the Pill from any page to reveal your navigation hub. The Dashboard gives you instant access to your most important data.

Tabs

Switch between your open browsing sessions.

Bookmarks

Access your saved favorite sites and folders.

History

View and search your recently visited pages.

Settings

Configure every detail of your browsing experience.

Secondary Menu: Tap the "Menu" button on the far right of the Dashboard to access Power Tools like Reader Mode, QR Tools, and Desktop Site toggles.

Recent History

Access your recent visits without leaving the search experience. When the search pill is focused, a dedicated handle appears.

The Handle

When typing, look for the small handle directly above the Search Pill.

Drag Up

Grab the handle and drag upwards to reveal a quick-access list of your most recent searches and visits.

Close

Drag the handle back down or tap the close button to return to the keyboard.

Instant Navigate

Tap any item in the list to immediately navigate to that page.

Auto-Expand: You can enable 'Show Recent History' in Settings to automatically open this drawer every time you focus the Pill.

Tab Management

Manage multiple sessions with ease. mi. uses high-fidelity snapshots so you never lose your place.

Grid/Card View

Switch between dense rows or large visual cards in Settings.

Reordering

In the tab view, long-press a tab and drag it to a new position to reorder your workspace.

Closing Tabs

Swipe left on a tab row or tap the 'X' on a card to close it. Use 'Clear All' to reset completely.

Tab Editing

Tap the pencil icon on any tab to change its display name or toggle whether the website favicon is shown.

Visual Snapshots

Real-time previews of your pages help you identify the right tab in seconds.

Bookmark Management

Keep your favorite corners of the web organized. mi. Browser provides a flexible bookmarking system with folder support.

Quick Save

Open the Dashboard Menu and select 'Bookmark' to instantly save the current page to your library.

Swipe to Edit

Swipe left on any bookmark or folder to reveal Edit and Delete actions. You can change names, URLs, or move items between folders.

Nested Folders

Create and manage folders to group related bookmarks together for easier access.

Instant Search

Quickly find any saved bookmark by typing in the search bar at the top of the Bookmarks view.

History Management

Never lose a page you've visited. mi. keeps a local-only record of your browsing activity that stays entirely on your device.

Search History

Filter through your past visits using the search bar within the History view.

Partial Deletion

Swipe left on any history item to remove it individually without affecting the rest of your data.

Clear Ranges

Wipe your history for the last hour, day, week, or all time via the trash icon in History.

Local-Only

Your history is never uploaded to any cloud. It is stored securely on your local file system.

Settings

Every aspect of mi. Browser can be customized. Here is a breakdown of every available setting:

Pro Tip: Most settings apply in real-time. You can change your accent color or UI spacing and see the results instantly without restarting the app.

Look & Feel

Theme Mode

Choose between Light, Dark, or Adaptive. Adaptive mode generates a custom theme based on your Accent Color.

Accent Color

Pick from over 30 hand-selected colors that define the app's UI elements, buttons, and highlights.

Show Status Bar

Toggles the visibility of your device's system status bar (clock, battery, etc.) for a more immersive look.

Interface Geometry

Corner Radius

Choose between Square (0px), Soft (10px), or Round (22px) for all UI components and cards.

Font Size Scaling

Adjust the global font scale from 80% to 120% to suit your readability needs.

UI Spacing

Select Compact, Normal, or Airy to adjust the padding and density of the interface.

Pill Height

Fine-tune the height of the interaction pill (60px to 80px) for optimal thumb reach.

Pill Loading Bar

Choose the progress bar style: Standard (Left-to-Right), Center Out, or Hidden.

Show Recent History

Toggles whether your most recent searches are automatically expanded when opening the Pill.

Tab Preferences

Tab View Mode

Toggle between Rows (dense list) or Cards (visual grid).

Show Tab Logo

Toggles the visibility of website icons (favicons) in the tab switcher.

Show Tab Preview

Enables real-time snapshots of your pages when using Card view.

Startup Behavior

Choose to start with a New Tab or Continue Session (restore all previously open tabs).

Browsing & Privacy

Search Engine

Support for Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, Baidu, Yahoo, and Yandex.

Background Refresh

Keeps your open tabs 'alive' in the background for instant switching (uses more battery).

HTTPS Only

Forces the browser to only connect to websites via secure encrypted connections.

Block Cookies

Prevents websites from storing tracking cookies on your device.

JavaScript Toggle

Optionally disable JavaScript for maximum security or to bypass certain site restrictions.

Data & History

History Load Count

Limit how many history items are displayed at once (10, 25, 50, or 100).

Clear History

Wipe your browsing data for the last hour, last 24 hours, last 7 days, or all time.

Reset All Settings

Reverts all customizations back to their factory defaults.

Power Tools

Tap the Menu button on the Dashboard to access advanced utility tools for any page.

Home Button

Instantly return to the minimalist home screen and reset your current session.

Quick Bookmark

Save the current page to your library with one tap from the Power Tools menu.

QR Toolbox

Scan physical codes, upload images from your gallery to scan, or generate a code for the current URL.

Reader Mode

Removes ads and clutter, leaving only the text and essential images for a clean reading experience.

Desktop Mode

Requests the desktop version of the current site for full functionality.

Share & Print

Native integration with your device's share sheet and wireless printing capabilities.

Privacy by Design

mi. Browser is not just minimal in design, but minimal in data collection. Your browsing remains yours.

No Tracking

We do not track your history, cookies, or search patterns. No data is sent to our servers.

Local Storage

All bookmarks and history are stored locally on your device using industry-standard encryption.
