The Complete User Guide

A comprehensive deep-dive into every feature, setting, and interaction within mi. Browser.

Pill Interactions

mi. Browser is designed for one-handed use. Everything centers around "The Pill" at the bottom of your screen.

Tap the Pill

Focuses the address bar. Type a URL or a search query to navigate instantly.

Swipe Up

Opens the Tab Switcher. The page shrinks into its card as your finger moves, like a phone's recent apps screen.

Swipe Down

Hides the Pill entirely for an immersive full-screen experience. Tap or swipe up on the floating recall button to bring it back.

Move the Recall Button

While the Pill is hidden, swipe the recall button left or right. It follows your finger and pins to the left, center, or right of the screen, wherever you let go.

Incognito

On a fresh tab, tap the mask in the Pill to make it incognito (a tab you've browsed in keeps its mode). Each incognito tab has its own private storage: nothing comes in from your other tabs and nothing leaves. Its pages aren't added to history, screenshots are blocked, the tab switcher shows it only as 'Incognito tab', and its cookies, logins and site data are erased when you close it. Long-press a link and choose 'Open in Incognito Tab' to send it there. While incognito tabs are open, a notification closes them all with one tap. In incognito the keyboard is asked not to learn what you type, passkeys can't be created (signing in with one works), and search suggestions are off unless you turn on Suggestions in Incognito. Downloads and bookmarks you choose to save are kept.

Search Widget

Add the mi widget to your home screen: tap the bar to open a new tab ready to type, or its buttons for a new incognito tab or the QR scanner. Long-press mi's icon for the same shortcuts.

Search, Never Open

Start with !se to always search your search engine, even for something that looks like an address: !se example.com searches for it instead of opening the site.

Navigation Gestures

Swipe Right

Go back to the previous page in your history.

Swipe Left

Go forward to the next page if you've navigated back.

Pro Tip: These gestures work directly on the Pill area, allowing for effortless one-handed browsing.

The Tab Switcher

Swipe up on the Pill from any page to open the Tab Switcher. Your current tab sits on the far right; swipe right to go back through the tabs you used before. The card's title and icon appear as soon as you start swiping. The bar at the bottom has a new tab button (+) and the ⋮ tab menu, with Bookmarks on the left and Settings on the right.

Tabs

Swipe between cards and tap one to zoom back into it. Swipe a card up to close it (with undo). Pinned tabs show a pin and can't be swiped away.

Library

The left button opens Bookmarks and History on top of the switcher; closing it takes you back.

New Tab

The + button opens a fresh tab and zooms into it.

Settings

The right button opens Settings on top of the switcher.

Tab Menu: Tap ⋮ for the centered tab: Favorite, Bookmark and Pin; Desktop site, Reader mode and Find in page tiles; QR tools, Print and Add to Home screen; copy link and share. "Close all tabs" keeps pinned tabs.

Expanded Search

Access your history and favorite sites without leaving the search experience. When the search pill is focused, an expanded interaction area appears.

Recent Searches

Drag the handle above the focused Pill upwards to reveal your recent searches. Tap one to put it back in the address bar.

Search Suggestions

While the drawer is open, suggestions from your search engine appear as you type, after your own matching past searches. The drawer never opens by itself.

Favorites Bar

A persistent row of your top 5 pinned sites sits at the bottom of the drawer for instant navigation.

Adding Favorites

Tap the '+' icon in the Favorites Bar to instantly pin the current page to your quick-access list.

Rearrange Favorites

Touch and drag any favorite icon to reorder them instantly.

Drag to Delete

Drag a favorite icon upwards out of the bar area to remove it.

Configurable: Use settings to automatically expand history or keep the Favorites Bar visible every time you focus the Pill.

Tab Management

Moving between tabs feels like switching apps: the live page zooms out into its card under your finger, and zooms back in when you pick a tab.

Closing Tabs

Swipe a card up. It resists until you pass the threshold (you'll feel a tick), then follows your finger; let go to close. Tap Undo if you didn't mean to. 'Close all tabs' is in the ⋮ menu.

Tab Editing

Long-press a card to rename the tab.

Live Previews

Every card shows a snapshot of its page, updated whenever you leave the tab.

Bookmark Management

Keep your favorite corners of the web organized. mi. Browser provides a flexible bookmarking system with folder support.

Quick Save

Open the Tab Switcher's ⋮ menu and tap Bookmark to save the centered tab to a folder of your choice.

Swipe to Edit

Swipe left on any bookmark or folder to reveal Edit and Delete actions. You can change names, URLs, or move items between folders.

Nested Folders

Create and manage folders to group related bookmarks together for easier access.

Instant Search

Quickly find any saved bookmark by typing in the search bar at the top of the Bookmarks view.

History Management

Never lose a page you've visited. mi. keeps a local-only record of your browsing activity that stays entirely on your device.

Search History

Filter through your past visits using the search bar within the History view.

Partial Deletion

Swipe left on any history item to remove it individually without affecting the rest of your data.

Clear Ranges

Wipe your history for the last hour, day, week, four weeks, or all time via the ⋮ menu in History.

Local-Only

Your history is never uploaded to any cloud. It is stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

Settings

Every aspect of mi. Browser can be customized. Settings opens on a list of sections; tap one to open its page, or search to find any setting from any section. Here is a breakdown of every available setting:

Pro Tip: Most settings apply in real-time. You can change your accent color or UI spacing and see the results instantly without restarting the app.

Colors

Theme

Choose Light, Dark, or System (follows your device's dark mode).

Pure Black

In dark mode, use true black backgrounds. Saves battery on OLED screens; cards and the Pill stay a shade lighter.

Accent Color

Adaptive (your wallpaper colors, Android 12+), Gray, Blue, Green, Red, Orange, Yellow, or Purple. The full Material 3 color scheme is generated from your choice.

Interface

Font Size

Adjust the global font scale from 80% to 120% to suit your readability needs.

Rounded Font

Rounds the stroke ends of Google Sans Flex across the whole interface. The mi. logo is always rounded.

Corners

Choose Square (0dp), Soft (12dp), or Round (28dp) for sheets, cards, and the Pill.

Spacing

Select Compact, Normal, or Airy to adjust the padding and density of the interface.

Status Bar

Toggles the visibility of your device's system status bar (clock, battery, etc.) for a more immersive look.

Darken Web Pages

In dark theme, darkens websites that don't have a dark mode of their own.

Pill

Loading Bar

Choose the progress bar style: Standard (Left-to-Right), Center Out, or Hidden.

Pin Favorites

Keeps the Favorites Bar visible at the bottom of the search drawer whenever the Pill is focused.

Expand Searches

Toggles whether your most recent searches are automatically expanded when opening the Pill.

Home Page

Center Logo

Choose the logo style displayed at the top of the home page: None, Static, or Fidget (interactive).

Clock

Select the clock format on the home page: None, 12h, or 24h.

Date

Choose the date placement on the home page: None, Above Clock, or Below Clock.

Shortcut

Toggle the home page shortcut button on or off. When enabled, choose its action: New Tab, QR Scanner, Bookmarks, or History.

Tabs

Site Logo

Toggles the visibility of website icons (favicons) in the tab switcher. When disabled, a globe icon is shown.

Background Refresh

When enabled, all tabs render on app startup instead of when opened. This allows for instant switching but uses more battery.

History

Group By

Organize your history by Time (Today, Yesterday) or by Site (hostname).

Load Count

The number of history items rendered at a time (10, 25, 50, or 100). Rendering more items may affect performance.

Clear History

Wipe your browsing data for the last hour, 24 hours, 7 days, 4 weeks, or all time.

Bookmarks

Site Logo

Toggles the visibility of website icons (favicons) in the bookmarks list. When disabled, a letter avatar is shown.

Search & Browsing

Search Engine

Support for Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Brave, Ecosia, and Yahoo.

Custom Search Engine

Pick Custom… under Search Engine and enter a name and address, with %s where your search goes (for example https://www.startpage.com/sp/search?query=%s). Custom engines don't offer suggestions.

Search Shortcuts

Start a search with a shortcut to search one site: !w Wikipedia, !yt YouTube, !r Reddit, !a Amazon, !m Maps, !i Images, and !se your search engine. Only one shortcut, at the very start. It turns into a small pill in the Pill once you type a space after it. Settings → Search Shortcuts lists them all and lets you add your own (a short name plus the site's search address, with %s where the search goes); yours take priority.

Pull to Refresh

Pull down at the very top of a page to reload it. It never triggers while you're scrolled down, even if you scroll back to the top in the same pull.

Search Suggestions

Shows suggestions from your search engine while you type. Turn off to keep keystrokes from being sent before you search.

Block Video Autoplay

Videos wait for a tap instead of playing on their own.

Default Browser

Make mi. the app that opens links on your phone.

Page Text Size

Text size on websites, from 50% to 200%, separate from the app's own Font Size.

Desktop Site by Default

Request desktop versions of websites. Each tab can still switch from the tab menu.

Reader Mode by Default

Simplify pages for reading. Each tab can still switch from the tab menu.

On Startup

Choose to start with a New Tab or Continue Session (restores your last browsing session).

Privacy & Security

Enable JavaScript

Optionally disable JavaScript for maximum security or to bypass certain site restrictions.

Enable Cookies

Allow websites to store cookies on your device. Disabling this may break login functionality on many sites.

Not Secure Marker

Pages without an encrypted connection (plain http, except your own device's localhost) and sites you allowed past a certificate warning show a red Not secure marker in the search pill. Tap it for details, and don't enter passwords or payment details there. Sign-in prompts on such sites warn that the password would be sent unencrypted.

HTTPS Only

Forces the browser to only connect to websites via secure encrypted connections. Local addresses are excluded: localhost, IP addresses, and local network names such as nas:5000, myapp.local or dev.test.

Block Ads

Blocks ads and ad networks with EasyList and the StevenBlack malware and ad domain list: matching requests are dropped and leftover ad spaces are hidden. On by default.

Block Trackers

Blocks analytics and tracking scripts that follow you across sites (EasyPrivacy). On by default.

Hide Cookie Banners

Hides cookie-consent pop-ups with the EasyList Cookie List. Turn it off if a site won't let you continue without answering its banner.

Remove Tracking from Links

Strips tracking tags such as utm_source, fbclid and gclid from links you open, copy or share. On by default.

Allowed Sites

Sites where ad, tracker and cookie-banner blocking is off. Add one from the tab menu on that site (switch off Block ads here); remove it here.

Update Filter Lists

The lists update themselves weekly on Wi-Fi; tap to update them now. Shows when they were last updated.

Block Third-Party Cookies

Stops sites embedded in a page from setting cookies that follow you across the web. Off by default, as in Chrome, because payment and sign-in windows (such as PayPal) rely on them.

Pop-ups

Pop-up windows open when you tap something (sign-in and payment windows stay linked to the page that opened them). Pop-ups without a tap are blocked; choose Always allow when asked to allow a site, and review or reset the list here.

Clear Browsing Data

Deletes cookies, site storage, cached files, saved site icons and tab snapshots, plus this session\'s sign-ins, permissions and certificate exceptions. You will be signed out of websites.

Backup & Reset

Export Settings

Save all your settings to a shareable file for backup or device migration.

Import Settings

Restore settings from a previously exported file. This replaces your current configuration.

Reset All Settings

Revert every setting to factory defaults without affecting your bookmarks, history, or tabs.

Check for Updates

Look for a new release now (in About). mi also checks once each time it starts.

Wipe All Data

Permanently delete all app data including bookmarks, history, tabs, favorites, and settings.

Power Tools

Tap ⋮ in the Tab Switcher for the centered tab's actions, modes and tools.

Favorite, Bookmark & Pin

The row at the top: add the page to your favorites bar, save it to a bookmark folder, or pin the tab so it can't be swiped away or closed by Close all tabs.

Copy & Share

Next to the tab's name: copy its link, or send it with your device's share sheet.

Find in Page

Search for specific text within the current webpage. Highlighting and navigation between matches included.

QR Toolbox

Scan physical codes, upload images from your gallery to scan, or generate a code for the current URL.

Reader Mode

Removes ads and clutter, leaving only the text and essential images for a clean reading experience.

Desktop Mode

Requests the desktop version of the current site for full functionality.

Share & Print

Native integration with your device's share sheet and Android's print service (print or save as PDF).

Privacy by Design

mi. Browser is not just minimal in design, but minimal in data collection. Your browsing remains yours.

No Tracking

We do not track your history, cookies, or search patterns. No data is sent to our servers.

Local Storage

Bookmarks, history, and tabs are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Sites you allow past certificate warnings, and camera, microphone or location permissions you grant, last only until you close the app.
